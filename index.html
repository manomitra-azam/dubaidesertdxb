Do I need a driving licence? +

For most guided off-road buggy and quad experiences, a local driving licence is not usually required. Age and safety rules vary by machine, so always confirm the exact package requirements before payment.

What should I wear in the desert? +

Comfortable breathable clothing and closed shoes are best for riding. Bring sunglasses and a light layer during cooler months or evening tours.

Is hotel pickup included? +

Selected desert safari packages include pickup and drop-off. Self-drive activity packages usually meet directly at the desert location unless a transfer add-on is chosen.

Can children join? +

Yes, but minimum age and passenger rules depend on the activity and vehicle. We recommend confirming children's ages with the team before booking.
